About Runaway Log

What is Runaway Log?

Runaway Log is a simple and intuitive activity tracking application designed to help you stay motivated and track your daily running or exercise activities.

With Runaway Log, you can:

  • Track your daily running activities with a simple tap
  • View your activity history in a beautiful calendar view
  • Get motivated with inspirational quotes
  • Play fun games to keep your motivation high
  • Customize the app with different themes and accent colors
  • Export and import your data for backup and sharing

Features

  • Simple and intuitive interface
  • Beautiful calendar view to track your activity history
  • Real-time statistics and insights
  • Motivational quotes to keep you inspired
  • Fun games to keep your motivation high
  • Customizable themes and accent colors
  • Export and import functionality
  • Responsive design for all devices
  • Multi-language support
